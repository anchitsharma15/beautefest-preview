Your privacy.

This page explains how the BeauteFest website handles enquiries and links to other services.

Enquiry form

The form prepares a message in your browser using the product category, optional occasion date, name and details you choose to enter. This website does not save your form entries to a database or send them to BeauteFest automatically. You can review the message before choosing “Continue on WhatsApp”. An enquiry does not place an order or confirm an appointment.

Choosing that link shares the prepared message with WhatsApp to open a draft. You then decide whether to send it. Information you send through WhatsApp, Instagram or a phone call is handled through that service and by the BeauteFest team. Share only what is needed to discuss your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own tracking cookies or use an analytics service. GitHub Pages may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps open those services. Their own privacy policies apply. Photos and the owner-supplied experience certificate are hosted with this website. Instagram videos load only after you choose “Load Instagram video”. This connects your browser to Instagram, which may process technical information and use cookies under its own privacy policy. You can instead choose “Watch on Instagram”.

Questions about an enquiry

To ask how the team handles information you have shared, or to request a correction or deletion, contact BeauteFest on +91 99141 93973 or through @beaute_fest on Instagram.
