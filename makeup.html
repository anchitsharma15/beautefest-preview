A little clarity

Find your
preferred finish.

These terms describe different aspects of makeup. Your artist can help you choose based on your skin, preferences and occasion.

Classic (Traditional)

Also called “Normal makeup”: conventional application using brushes or sponges, with the look discussed during your consultation.

HD

A focus on a carefully blended, camera-ready finish. HD products can be applied with conventional tools; HD does not mean airbrush.

Airbrush

Complexion makeup applied in fine layers using an airbrush device. The final finish depends on the formula, preparation and application.

HD describes a finish or product approach; airbrush describes an application method. Neither guarantees a particular wear time or suits every skin type. Discuss your skin needs and a trial with the artist.
